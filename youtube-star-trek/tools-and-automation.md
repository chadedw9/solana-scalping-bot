# Tools & Automation

This workspace has some genuinely useful AI/creator tools wired in via MCP. Here
is what's available and how each one fits the channel workflow. (You ask me, and
I drive these tools for you.)

## 🎨 AI Image & Video Generation

A creative-media server is connected with tools for:

- **`generate_image`** — Make thumbnails, channel art, B-roll stills, and
  illustrative visuals (e.g., "a dramatic starship silhouette over a nebula,
  cinematic, no logos"). Great for avoiding copyrighted screenshots.
- **`generate_video`** — Generate short B-roll/establishing clips (starfields,
  abstract warp effects, sci-fi vibes) to cut between your voiceover.
- **`reframe`** — Reframe a 16:9 video to 9:16 for **Shorts** automatically.
- **`upscale_video`** — Bump resolution/quality of a clip.
- **`personal_clipper`** — Auto-cut long footage into short clips (useful for
  spinning a 10-min episode into Shorts).
- **`virality_predictor`** — Score a video/concept for hook strength, retention
  risk, and predicted engagement *before* you publish. Use this to A/B your
  intros and thumbnails.
- **`video_analysis`** — Analyze a video's content/performance signals.

**How to use it:** Just ask me, e.g.
> "Generate 3 thumbnail backgrounds for episode 1 — a latinum bar with a red no-
> slash, cinematic."
> "Make a 6-second warp-streak B-roll clip."
> "Run the virality predictor on my episode 1 hook."

> ⚠️ These tools may consume credits on the connected account. I'll flag before
> doing anything that burns a lot, and we can check the balance first.

## 📅 Calendar

A calendar server is connected. We can use it to enforce your publishing
cadence:
- Create recurring **"Publish Day"** events
- Block **batch-record** and **batch-write** sessions
- Set reminders so you never miss your weekly upload

> "Put a recurring 'Publish Star Trek video' event every Tuesday at 5pm, and a
> 'Batch record' block the first Saturday of each month."
