# Episode Idea Backlog

30+ ready-to-shoot episode concepts. Each has a **working title** (optimized for
click + search) and a **hook** (the question the video answers). Titles use
proven patterns: *Explained, Why, Ranked, The Truth About, Everything Wrong
With, How X Actually Works.*

> ⭐ = strong "first 5 episodes" pick — broad appeal, evergreen, easy B-roll.

## Lore deep-dives (flagship "Stardate Files")

1. ⭐ **Why the Federation Has No Money — Explained** — How does a post-scarcity
   economy actually work, and does it hold up?
2. ⭐ **The Prime Directive Is a Lie** — How often Starfleet breaks its own
   #1 rule, and why the rule is contradictory.
3. ⭐ **What Is the Q Continuum, Really?** — Godlike beings explained without the
   headache.
4. **How Warp Drive Actually Works (In-Universe)** — Warp fields, subspace, and
   why you can't just "go faster."
5. **The Borg, Explained: From Threat to Joke and Back** — Their evolution
   across series and why they got less scary.
6. **Why Vulcans Aren't Actually Emotionless** — The most misunderstood species
   in Trek.
7. **Section 31: Starfleet's Secret Dirty Hands** — The black-ops org and what
   it says about the Federation.
8. **The Mirror Universe, Explained** — Every appearance, how you get there, and
   the rules.
9. **What Happened to Earth Before Star Trek?** — WWIII, First Contact, and the
   road to utopia.
10. **The Klingon Empire: A Complete History** — From TOS bumpy-head retcon to

## How to prioritize

For your **first 5**, pick the ⭐ episodes. They are:
- **Broad** (don't require deep fandom to click)
- **Evergreen** (earn views for years)
- **Searchable** ("explained", "ranked", "why")
- **Easy to illustrate** with B-roll you can generate or create

Then let the data lead: whichever of the first 5 over-performs, make 3 more in
that lane.
